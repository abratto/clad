# Maintenance change — `v0.14.0` CLAD hardening

- **Rulebook:** `methodology/core/ITERATIVE_CHANGES.md`
- **Change class:** `platform`
- **Status:** `closed`
- **Affected profile(s):** all (quality-gate, git hooks, generators, templates, methodology)
- **Feature-contract impact:** `preserved`
- **Design gate:** `approved`
- **Evidence gate:** `approved`
- **Change summary:** remediate the 45 deficits (D1–D45) surfaced by the
  four-use-case `clad-library-lending-v2` v0.13.0 experiment. One MAJOR-none /
  MINOR `0.14.0` bundle across four workstreams: (A) unblock derived projects at
  commit time, (B) close Model-B false-green gaps, (C) fix generator/convention
  rework multipliers, (D) docs/templates/examples plus the two process gaps.

## Mechanism

Source of truth: the experiment ledger
(`clad-library-lending-v2/docs/experiment-log.md`, D1–D45), located against the
CLAD source during planning.

- **WS-A (derived-project unblock).** `verify_iterative_change_coupling.py#has_active_iterative_change`
  scopes R17 coupling to iterative changes (an active `_changes/` record), so
  ordinary feature-stage commits are governed by readiness/sequence, not by
  same-commit spec+impl pairing; `#changed_concept_impls`/`#changed_sync_impls`
  exempt bootstrap concepts and non-implementation classes, pair only on
  modification via `#exists_at_base`, and `#changed_files` drops the unstaged
  surface. `verify_maintenance_change_readiness.py#is_project_config_only`
  scopes derived-project binding-key edits out. `clad#active_feature` refuses to
  guess a completed feature. `verify_relational_mapping.py` gains its exec bit.
- **WS-B (Model-B correctness).** `promote_concepts.py#_catalog_text` renders the
  catalog against the pending write set; `artifact_parsers.py#expected_stage_outputs`
  derives the 04b manifest independently of the promotion-erased state diff;
  `clad_stages.py#_concept_source_requires` stops first-feature skips;
  `_CONCEPT_ADDITIVITY` runs at Stage 02; `verify_reused_concept_contracts.py`
  guards reused binds; `advance.py#main` runs the full-pipeline pre-flight.
- **WS-C (generators/conventions).** `generate_feature_files.py#_terminal_branches`
  emits one Scenario per terminal branch; `generate_syncs.py#_split_payload_fields`
  and `#_respond_status` fix payload lowering and respond literals;
  `verify_step_definition_parity.py#_EXPR_TO_REGEX` allows signed numbers;
  `verify_concept_field_assertions.py#is_refusal_test` is case-insensitive;
  `verify_concept_state_relational.py#HEADER_RE` is MULTILINE.
- **Outcome discipline.** `verify_outcome_casing.py` pins authored outcome
  tokens to SCREAMING_SNAKE_CASE at Stages 01b/02 (D9/D26);
  `verify_distinct_outcomes.py` fails when one completion token fans out to two
  terminal responses, requiring distinct pure guard actions (D3);
  `verify_concept_test_derivation.py#main` compares outcomes under
  `normalize_outcome`.
- **WS-D (docs/templates/process).** Contradiction and staleness fixes across
  stage CONTEXTs and templates, plus the policy-value (D39) and cross-use-case
  addressability (D40) checks in the Stage-01 contract.

## Gates

### Design gate

Approved by the operator in-session: one `0.14.0` bundle, one maintenance
record, derived-project `clad.properties` scoped out, R17 pairs only on
modification, D39/D40 handled inside CLAD, 2-UC smoke accepted.

### Evidence gate

Approved on a green `quality-gate/tests` suite and `./clad verify`, plus a
derived-project surface smoke against this branch with the real pre-commit hook
installed. The smoke reproduced every previously-blocking commit and confirmed
the fixes:

- `clad.properties` binding-key edit commits with no maintenance record (D1).
- A greenfield commit adding the `Web` bootstrap, a concept impl, a
  concept test, a `*SyncRules` aggregator, a `SyncTestFixture`, and a sync impl
  passes the hook with **no `CLAD_HOOK_SKIP`** (D29/D30/D31).
- Modifying an existing concept impl without its spec commits cleanly during
  feature-stage work (D31); an unstaged impl no longer blocks a staged commit
  (D42).
- `verify_outcome_casing.py` rejects a PascalCase chain and accepts SCREAMING
  (D9/D26); `verify_distinct_outcomes.py` rejects a single `REFUSED` token
  reaching 400 and 409 (D3).

The full 4-UC end-to-end re-run was replaced by this surface smoke with the
operator's agreement.
